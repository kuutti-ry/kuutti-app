# matching

Rounds, filters, likes, budgets, recycling. TD-11, TD-12, TD-14.

#94 (ADR-015): `pool.ts` is who can be shown to whom, `inEachOthersPool`: every hard filter in both directions, symmetric by construction. The pond gate counts pools with it and the round builder (#95) draws candidates through it. A block of either side excludes there (#84, ADR-030 §2): each person carries the identities they blocked. So does a deal-breaker of either side (#87, ADR-015 §15): each person carries their own answers and their deal-breakers, and one on a field its holder has not answered is paused. `deal-breakers.ts` (#149) stores the deal-breakers as hard rows on the registry's whitelist, each only on a field the person answered themselves, and reads the rows back (`dealBreakersFrom`) for the person and for the gate's count alike. `soft.ts` (#87, ADR-019) is `softMatches`, the count of soft fields on which two people are similar from both sides: the round's order, never a filter, and nothing else reads it. `savePreferences` calls the pond's `admissionAnew`: whom one seeks decides with whom one waits at the gate (ADR-015 §9).

#96 (ADR-034): `rounds.ts` is today's round: the two tables' statements, `saveRound` (the round builder's one writer, #95), the read through the card builder `app.ts` hands in (`CardOf`), the seen report and the decision (changeable until the Finnish day ends, under the round row's lock), erasure's and the export's halves, and the four research events. Every write asks the block and the candidate's state in the statement that writes (ADR-030 §2). `round-routes.ts` is the app's three routes, the day in the path of each write.

#95 (ADR-035), the first cut: `round-order.ts` is `roundOrder`, the places of a round no rule forces: everybody in each other's pool both ways, minus whom a hidden field keeps apart (`hiddenByField`, ADR-019 §5), the most soft matches first, ties in an order of the day's and the person's, up to `round_size`. The builder itself reads across slices and lives with the jobs (`jobs/round-builder.ts`): who gets a round, the cooldowns, the conversations, the night after the gate's count, and the day's first read, handed into `round-routes.ts` by `app.ts` (`BuildToday`).

#98's budget half (ADR-036): `decide` counts the day's likes under the round's lock, `like_budget_balanced` for everybody; a like back on a `liked_you` card and a pass are free; over the budget, 429 `like_budget_exceeded` with the wait until midnight.
