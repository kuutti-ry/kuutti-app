# matching

Rounds, filters, likes, budgets, recycling. TD-11, TD-12, TD-14.

#94 (ADR-015): `pool.ts` is who can be shown to whom, `inEachOthersPool`: every hard filter in both directions, symmetric by construction. The pond gate counts pools with it and the round builder (#95) draws candidates through it. A block of either side excludes there (#84, ADR-030 §2): each person carries the identities they blocked. So does a deal-breaker of either side (#87, ADR-015 §15): each person carries their own answers and their deal-breakers, and one on a field its holder has not answered is paused. `deal-breakers.ts` (#149) stores the deal-breakers as hard rows on the registry's whitelist, each only on a field the person answered themselves, and reads the rows back (`dealBreakersFrom`) for the person and for the gate's count alike. `soft.ts` (#87, ADR-019) is `softMatches`, the count of soft fields on which two people are similar from both sides: the round's order, never a filter, and nothing else reads it. `savePreferences` calls the pond's `admissionAnew`: whom one seeks decides with whom one waits at the gate (ADR-015 §9).

#96 (ADR-034): `rounds.ts` is today's round: the two tables' statements, `saveRound` (the round builder's one writer, #95), the read through the card builder `app.ts` hands in (`CardOf`), the seen report and the decision (changeable until the Finnish day ends, under the round row's lock), erasure's and the export's halves, and the research events (`round_built`, `profile_viewed`, `like`, `pass`, and `match` since #100). Every write asks the block and the candidate's state in the statement that writes (ADR-030 §2). `round-routes.ts` is the app's three routes, the day in the path of each write.

#95 (ADR-035), the first cut: `round-order.ts` is `roundOrder`, the places of a round no rule forces: everybody in each other's pool both ways, minus whom a hidden field keeps apart (`hiddenByField`, ADR-019 §5), the most soft matches first, ties in an order of the day's and the person's, up to `round_size`. The builder itself reads across slices and lives with the jobs (`jobs/round-builder.ts`): who gets a round, the cooldowns, the conversations, the night after the gate's count, and the day's first read, handed into `round-routes.ts` by `app.ts` (`BuildToday`).

#98's budget half (ADR-036): `decide` counts the day's likes under the round's lock, `like_budget_balanced` for everybody; a like back on a `liked_you` or `fish` card and a pass are free; over the budget, 429 `like_budget_exceeded` with the wait until midnight.

#97 (ADR-037): `likedYouPlaces` is the round's first places, the people whose like waits on the person in the order the builder reads them (the oldest like first), up to `liked_you_cap`, each one somebody the person may meet. The builder reads the waiting likes from the round record itself (`waitingOn` in `jobs/round-builder.ts`): likes of ended days within `like_expiry_days`, not answered. A shadow-banned person's round holds none of these places (ADR-037 §7).

#100 (ADR-038): `decide` makes a match of a like meeting a standing like, under a lock on the pair, opening the conversation through the chat slice's `openConversation` (handed in by `app.ts` as `Matches`), and answers with it; a like that is part of a match is final (409 `match_made`). After the commit, `Matches.made` hands the match to the push, which tells the person whose earlier like it met (#102, ADR-055 §5). `unopenedRoundsOf` answers whom the round's push tells: today's rounds built and not opened, with a card that can still be shown, of people who opened a round in the last `round_push_idle_days` (ADR-055 §6, `jobs/round-push.ts`).

#378 (ADR-039): a fish is a like with a mark (`round_entries.fish`), chosen as `fish` on a card and read back so; `decide` counts the person's fish of the last `fish_window_days` under the round's lock (429 `fish_budget_exceeded`), and on a `liked_you` or `fish` card it is a like. The builder puts the waiting fish ahead of the waiting likes, labelled `fish`. #475: `fish_window_days` is counted in periods from a Monday, so its 7 is the calendar week, Monday to Sunday, and the round and each decision's answer carry `fishToSend`, whether the caller can send a fish today, from the same count as the refusal (`fishSent`) and an earned fish waiting; the phone offers the fish only while it is true, and on the card that holds the fish. #524 (ADR-036 §6): the round and each decision's answer carry `likesLeft`, the budget less the same count as the refusal (`likesSent`), a fish included, zero at the least; the phone says it in words.

#107 (ADR-044 §8): a like past the day's budget, or a fish past the window's, uses a unit the person's invitations earned instead of being refused, oldest first, in `decide`'s transaction under the round's lock; the counts leave out what a unit paid for, so a banked unit never fills the budget it was used past; a decision changed to a pass gives back every unit on its card, a fish made a like its fish's, while a like made a fish keeps its like's. Free likes spend nothing. The reward table is the rewards slice's: `decide` reaches it through `rewards/index.ts` (`spendEarned`, `releaseEarned`, `entriesPaidBy`, and `earnedFishWaiting` for `fishToSend`).

#99 (ADR-042): the short pool. The builder fills the ordered places with new people first and lets earlier ones come back after them (`comingBackOrder`), counting only rounds the person opened. A short round offers to relax one of the person's own deal-breakers for the day (`GET /rounds/{day}/relaxations`, counted on asking, in the gate's steps); a tap records it on the day's round (`round_relaxation`) and adds whom it lets in after the last place (`POST`, `appendRelaxed`, reason `relaxed` with `relaxed_field`), and one tap undoes it with the cards still undecided (`DELETE`, `undoRelaxation`). The pond is read by the builder (`relaxationOffers`, `relaxedCandidates` in `jobs/round-builder.ts`, handed in as `ShortPool`); `relaxedBy` (`pool.ts`) loosens only the person's side, so the other side's filters always hold. A like on a relaxed card waives the liker's deal-breakers that card came in under (`relaxed_with`) for its person when their round is built. Every card names the caller's own deal-breakers its person did not answer (`notAnswered`).
