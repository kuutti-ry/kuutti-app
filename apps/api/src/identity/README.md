# identity

Bank-ID registration, identity standing, accounts, sessions, re-registration. TD-1, TD-7. The Telia dialect itself is `@kuutti/tunnistus-oidc` (ADR-017); `oidc-broker.ts` is the adapter that keeps Kuutti's part of a login. Staff sessions (#49, ADR-006): the same bank login marked as a staff attempt resolves to an identity with a `moderator_roles` row and an eight-hour admin session (`admin-session.ts`), never to an account. Erasure and export (#51, ADR-007): `erasure.ts` runs the erasure table over what exists and builds the export; `account-routes.ts` serves both to the caller's own account. The research consent (#46, #50) also writes and removes the `research_subject` row through the research slice's surface, in the consent's own transaction. Taking a break (#170, ADR-022): `pause.ts` pauses the caller's account with its state kept aside and ends its sessions; `login.ts` ends the break at the next bank login and answers `unpaused`.
