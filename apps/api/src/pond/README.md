# pond

Ponds, the switch that opens matching in one, the public counter. TD-10, TD-13. #54 (ADR-013): `GET /ponds/counter` needs no session and serves, for every pond, the figures it stands on through `publishCounts` of `packages/schema` (nothing below k, with a floor of 10 under `matching_config.pond_counter_k`; the split only when every cell is at least k and the people without a declared gender are none or at least k; the people finishing only when they and the rest are none or at least k). `counter.ts` is the read. The counting is the nightly job `jobs/pond-counter.ts`, which reads across slices so that none depends on another for it, and moves a pond's figures only once at least k people have come or gone (`moveFigures`). `pond_counter` holds one row per pond and no history.

#507 (ADR-013 §12 to §16, §18): with a session, the same route also says on the caller's own pond whom its people seek, as percentages of three bands of finished profiles in steps of five through `seeksShares` of `packages/schema`, or null until every band is at least k. That answer is `no-store` and the session is checked as on any other route; with no session the route says no band at all. The job counts the bands, except in production until the consent's wording is settled, and `moveFigures` moves them on their own.

#500 (ADR-060): matching in a pond opens when an admin turns its switch on (`ponds.matching_open`, `admin-routes.ts`: `GET /admin/ponds`, `POST /admin/ponds/{id}/matching`, one `pond.matching` audit row a change). `GET /gate` tells the caller one of three things, incomplete, closed or open, handed its reader by the app: who is complete reads across slices and lives with the jobs (`jobs/pool.ts`). Who is in whose pool is `inEachOthersPool` of the matching slice.
