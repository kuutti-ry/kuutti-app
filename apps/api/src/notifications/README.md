# notifications

The switches, the notices in the app, the push of a message and of a match, optional email channel. TD-3, TD-18.

- `notifications.ts`, `repo.ts`, `routes.ts` (#176, ADR-032): what a person may be notified of, one switch per kind (`GET /account/notifications`, `PUT /account/notifications/{kind}`), on until switched off, stored as a row only once touched; and the notices in the app (`GET /notices`, `POST /notices/{id}/read`), what must reach a person even without a push, holding no text of their own. The first notice is a moderator's warning, written in the warning's transaction (`recordWarningNotice`, ADR-024 §7); a warning lifted since is not shown. The second is an invitation of the person's that counted (#107, ADR-044 §9), written by the night's count in its transaction (`recordInvitationNotices`), with what it earned them read from their own units of the invitation; never who was invited. The third is the invitation the person used, counted (#503, ADR-044 §9), written the same way, only when it earned them a fish; never who invited them. Erased with the account; in the export. The switches are read by the push below.
- `push.ts`, `tokens.ts` (#102, ADR-055): the push. `PUT /push-token` registers the caller's session's Expo token, worded in the request's language; one per session, a token in one session, taken from any other session that held it (the one statement here not scoped by the caller's account, and why is in place). A token goes when its session is revoked (the `session_revoked_push_token` trigger) or deleted, at erasure (`erasePushTokensOf`, before the sessions) and when Expo says the device is gone. `createPush` (one per process, made at boot) decides whom to tell: a message to its readers (the chat's `messageReaders`, handed in by `app.ts`) who have no live socket, once a conversation per `message_push_min_interval_minutes` (in memory); a match to the one whose earlier like it met (`Matches.made`). Both run after the answer that caused them (`settled` waits for them), so an answer's time says nothing of whether anybody was pushed. Each reads the switch, the quiet hours (`push_quiet_from_hour` to `push_quiet_to_hour` in Helsinki, each 0 to 23) and the tokens of live sessions, and sends one line of the app's words with `PushData`, the kind alone, through `Deps.pusher` (`lib/push.ts`): never a text, a name, an account or the conversation, and never living past the quiet hours' start or the day's end. Requests of a hundred go 200 ms apart, and one Expo refuses for a stray project's tokens drops them and goes again; a failure is logged by its kind. The export says which sessions have a token, never the token (`exportPushTokens`).
- `announcements.ts`, `admin-routes.ts` (#573, ADR-068): an announcement to everybody. In the panel an admin writes it, English and Finnish, and Swedish or not, under the plain-text rule, and another admin approves it (`POST /admin/announcements/{id}/approve`; the one who wrote it cannot, a check in the database holds it), which writes a notice for every account that is not erased in its transaction; any admin may delete a draft, and nothing takes a sent one back. Each act is an audit row with no words. After the commit `push.announcement` sends: the email to every confirmed address whose Announcements switch is on, read fresh just before each send, in the language the person read the terms in, ten a second at most (`mailAnnouncement`); and the push in the app's own words to everybody whose notice is unread, waiting in the process for the quiet hours to end if they hold. `GET /notices` gives the notice its words in the request's language (`announcementTextFor`): Swedish falling back to English, Stadi reading Finnish. A sent announcement is kept for good, staff's work, exported to nobody.
