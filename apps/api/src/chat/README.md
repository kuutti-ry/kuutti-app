# chat

Conversations, presence, contacts shared in a conversation, auto-archive. TD-3.

- `conversations.ts`, `messages.ts`, `repo.ts`: the list, one conversation, text messages, the read mark, erasure's and the export's halves (#348, ADR-031 §1 to §8).
- `photos.ts`: a photo in a message (#350, ADR-031 §10): one request uploads and sends through the media slice's pipeline and check, the photo and its message one transaction, the day's photos counted under the sender's lock and the day's tries in the process's memory, both to midnight in Finland. The photo's URLs ride on a page, the read of one message and the send's answer to its sender (`withPhotoUrls` in `messages.ts`), never on a socket frame.
- `contacts.ts`: contacts shared in a conversation (#103, ADR-031 §11). A person keeps up to four pieces (`/contacts`, `/contacts/{kind}`: phone, WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram, each in the one form `normaliseContact` gives), seen by nobody; one press shares a piece in an open conversation (`POST /conversations/{id}/contacts/{kind}`, in the send's order: the account held, the row locked, the state read afresh, then the piece held so a removal in flight ends what the share begins), and the other person reads it in the conversation's detail at once, until its owner stops it (`DELETE` on the same path), removes or changes the piece, or the conversation closes (`repo.closeConversationsOf` ends every share of the conversations it closes). The detail withholds a share across a close, a block, or a shadow ban still open when it began or when its piece last changed, as a message is withheld. `GET /conversations/{id}/contacts` answers the detail's contacts alone, by the same read, signing nothing and writing nothing: the phone reads it on a `contacts` frame. A share's row holds no value; erasure deletes the pieces and keeps the shares, ended, as the record. A removal holds the account first, as the keep and the share do, so it and an erasure never wait for each other in a cycle. The share, its end, the keep and the removal count against `messages_per_minute`. A share counts as activity for the night's archive, as a message does. Events `contact_shared` and `contact_unshared` carry neither kind nor value; the `contacts` frame names the conversation only, and reaches a phone only while the conversation is open between the two and the other is under no shadow ban still open.
- `ws.ts`, `live.ts`, `listen.ts`, `presence.ts`: the live socket (#349, ADR-031 §9).
- `archive.ts`: the night's archive of silent conversations (TD-13). One transaction locks the candidates' rows first (`repo.lockSilent`), waiting for any send or share that holds one, then archives those still silent on a fresh read (`repo.archiveSilent`), so a share that committed meanwhile keeps its conversation out; each conversation archived is an event for each linked person (`races.test.ts` holds the race on two connections).
