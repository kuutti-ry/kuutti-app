# safety

Reports, blocks, sanctions, the audit log. TD-5, TD-6. The audit log (`audit.ts`, #49, ADR-006) is the first piece: append-only by trigger, one row per staff action or view; the notes of the photo check (`photo-check.ts`, #253) are the second: which kind of photo the check did not show and when, the closer look two of them in 30 days start, nothing else; feedback from inside the app (`feedback.ts`, `feedback-routes.ts`, #171, ADR-023) is the third: a kind and a few lines a person sends, which the moderators read in the panel and mark handled with an audit row, the text in no log line and no event, five a day, erased with the account and listed in the export; sanctions (`sanctions.ts`, `sanction-routes.ts`, #307, ADR-024) are the fourth: warn, shadow ban, suspension until a day and ban, each a row with a reason and staff's note, lifted and looked at again by a second member of staff, a ban an admin's; the account's state and the identity's standing are settled from what is still open by the identity slice (`settleStanding`), and the night ends suspensions past their day; the tunables in the panel (`config.ts`, `config-routes.ts`, #304, #305) are the fifth: every key of `matching_config` with its versions for admins, and a new version with a reason, held to the current value's shape and written to the audit log; the panel's front page (`overview-routes.ts`, #303) is the sixth: each queue's count and the age of its oldest, with the API's clock and `moderation_response_hours`; a person's page (`account-routes.ts`, `accounts.ts`, #306) is the seventh: one account by id with its raw state, standing, the profile as the card shows it, photos, feedback and the audit rows about them, every open an `account.view` row; the audit log's reader (`audit-routes.ts`, #309) is the eighth: fifty rows a page by a keyset cursor to the microsecond, narrowed by actor, action, subject and a window, for admins; a read narrowed to one subject writes `audit.read` about it first, as the person's page does; the staff list (`staff-routes.ts`, #310) is the ninth: who holds a role, from whom and since when, and their last sign-in, read-only; reports and blocks from a card (`blocks.ts`, `reports.ts`, `report-routes.ts`, #84, ADR-030) are the tenth: a person blocks or reports somebody whose card they were shown, or with whom a block already stands between the two accounts, a report blocks too, the block is between the two identities and final, and it holds both ways wherever a person is shown to a person through `blocked_between` (migration 0051) and the pool; at most `reports_per_day` reports a day, one open per pair, the note in no log line or event; both outlive erasure (reports twelve months, pruned nightly), the export lists the person's own; nothing but the filters reads a block (`no-signal.test.ts`). The report queue and the decision are #311 and #312; reporting from a conversation waits for the chat (#101).
