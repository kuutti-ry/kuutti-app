# safety

Reports, blocks, sanctions, the audit log. TD-5, TD-6. The audit log (`audit.ts`, #49, ADR-006) is the first piece: append-only by trigger, one row per staff action or view; the notes of the photo check (`photo-check.ts`, #253) are the second: which kind of photo the check did not show and when, the closer look two of them in 30 days start, nothing else; feedback from inside the app (`feedback.ts`, `feedback-routes.ts`, #171, ADR-023) is the third: a kind and a few lines a person sends, which the moderators read in the panel and mark handled with an audit row, the text in no log line and no event, five a day, erased with the account and listed in the export; the rest arrives with the Safety milestone (#84).
