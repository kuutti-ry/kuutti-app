# media

Photo upload, variants, signed URL issuance (#48, TD-2, TD-8, ADR-005); moderation and the review queue (#49, ADR-006). The exposure budget and the shown record (#52, ADR-008) are `budget.ts` and the statements of `repo.ts`.

Two purposes share the table (#350, ADR-031 §10): `profile`, ordered and on the card, and `message`, a photo sent in a conversation, with no position. Every profile read asks for `purpose = 'profile'`, so a message photo never reaches a card, a photos screen, the queue or a conversation's thumb. The chat sends one through `prepareMessagePhoto` (the pipeline, the check on the full size before anything is stored, then the objects) and `storeMessagePhoto` in its own transaction; `issueMessagePhotoUrls` signs one for a reader until the next five-minute mark and fifteen minutes more, so its URLs live 15 to 20 minutes. On CloudFront a repeated read inside the five minutes gets the same URL; the compose stand-in presigns afresh each second, so there the URL changes, and since the app caches by photo id and variant, nothing depends on it. One statement per answer writes the fetch log, one row per viewer, photo and variant in each five-minute window, with the purpose `message` and never counted against the day's fetches; two reads at the same instant, in two transactions, may each write theirs. The chat asks for them in a page, the read of one message and the send's answer to its sender, never in a socket frame. `exportPhotos` puts the person's newest hundred sent photos in the export's zip (a `shortcut:`), each read logged with the purpose `message`, never counted.

- `pipeline.ts`: `inspectImage` reads format and size from the file header (the pixel cap applies before any decoder); `processPhoto` re-encodes with sharp into the three WebP variants, computes the content address and the blurhash. Nothing of the input survives it.
- `store.ts`: the object store behind `MediaStore` (S3 or the compose stand-in through `lib/s3.ts`; a Map in tests). Only variants reach `put`.
- `urls.ts`: `UrlSigner`, CloudFront canned-policy URLs in deployed environments, presigned GETs on the compose stand-in in development.
- `repo.ts`: the rows, every statement scoped by `account_id` (rule 6). `photos.ts`: the service. `routes.ts`: the six routes, the caption's among them (#296: `PUT /photos/{id}/caption`, a key of `PHOTO_CAPTIONS` or null, scoped like the rest). `wiring.ts`: what boot builds from the configuration.
- Tests: `pipeline.test.ts` on fixtures made at test time (`src/test/media.ts`), `urls.test.ts` verifies signatures with the public half, `routes.test.ts` runs every route through `app.request()` with a memory store, `store.local.test.ts` runs against the compose stand-in when `S3_ENDPOINT` is set (the compose job).
- `moderation.ts`: the `Moderator` seam (Rekognition through the instance role: three detect calls, or queue-everything where there is none), the pure decision over `matching_config` thresholds (moderation labels only), tip signals stored beside them for #56, `moderatePhoto` after the upload and `sweepPendingPhotos` nightly. Rekognition is sent a JPEG copy padded to at least 80 px on each side, its least size, so a tiny picture (a message photo's full size is never enlarged) is checked rather than refused as busy. Counts in the log line, never label names.
- `description.ts`: the automatic description of #296 (ADR-006 §1 and §3): the closed list of what DetectLabels is asked for and the face attributes DetectFaces is asked for by name (never ALL, GENDER, AGE_RANGE or EMOTIONS), `descriptionSignalsFrom` over Rekognition's answers and `photoDescriptionOf` at the line of `photo_description_confidence_min`, both pure. Kept as `photo_review.description`, keys of `PhotoDescription` (packages/schema) and never a sentence; rides on every `Photo` and `CardPhoto`, and the export adds the words (`describePhoto` of packages/i18n). The maintainer strikes or adds names here and in `messages.yaml` together. Tests in `moderation.test.ts`.
- `tips.ts`: the advisory tips of #56 and #253, `tips` and `fishSpecies` pure over a snapshot, and `photoTips`, which gathers it (the photos, their `photo_review` signals, the `matching_config` thresholds); answered on the photo list, `GET /photos` and `PUT /photos/order`, since #287. Advice only: never a moderation, completeness or matching decision. Tests: `tips.test.ts`, and the routes in `routes.test.ts`.
- `admin-routes.ts`: the queue, the card URL and the decision for staff, behind `requireAdmin` with an audit row per view and decision (`safety/audit.ts`).
- Tests: `moderation.test.ts` (the decision as a Scenario Outline, the upload path with a scripted moderator), `admin-routes.test.ts`; specs in `features/media/moderation.feature`.
