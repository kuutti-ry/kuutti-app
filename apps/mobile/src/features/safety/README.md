# safety

Reports, blocks, snapshots, shadow-ban rules, moderation actions. TD-5, TD-6.

Block and report from someone else's card (#84, ADR-030 §11):

- `SafetyActions.tsx`: the "Block or report" icon button (decorative icon, the label in words, 44 pt) and one dialog. Block, or Report and block, each with its line of what it does; a block is confirmed in words that say it is final, holds through either person's deletion and is not told; a report asks for one of #106's six reasons (a radio group, the chosen one marked in text) and, optionally, up to 500 characters, says that it blocks too, and sends. Once one went through, the dialog says so (Done, a name apart from the corner's Close), and closing it hands the card's account id to `onGone`; one that lands after the dialog was closed hands it at once and is said aloud on both platforms, since nothing on the screen says it, and a failure that lands then opens the dialog again where it was. A 404 (the card's person or its photos gone meanwhile) is said as "no longer available" and hands the card back too. The day's cap on reports (`report_budget_exceeded`) is said in words with a Block under it, without promising a report later; a failure, the scrim, the corner and the back gesture keep the reason and the words, and only Cancel forgets them. Each outcome is announced on iOS (Android has the live regions), and the focus moves to each step's title. The dialog is bounded by the window with its middle scrolling, and the note's keyboard goes with a drag or the return key; none of it is tried on a device until #96's flow.
- `client.ts`: `POST /blocks` and `POST /reports` through the typed client. Both answer 204 whatever stood before, so nothing here learns anything about the other person.
- `ProfileCardView` (profile slice) shows it beside the name only when its screen passes `onGone`, with the same as an accessibility action on the name; the owner's preview passes nothing. No screen shows anybody another person's card until the round screen (#96), which passes `onGone` and gets the Maestro flow. No router route and no deep link names another person's account.
