# matching

The queue, filters, likes, budgets, recycling. TD-11, TD-12, TD-14, ADR-062.

The queue (#379, #502, ADR-034, ADR-062): `RoundScreen`, `useRound`, the round's client; the route stays `/round` and the API's stay under `/rounds`, the day's log of it. `useRound` keeps the cards it holds, and once none is left to decide it reads on: what comes goes after them by place, so that Undo's last card stays, and only an empty answer is the queue's end. While that read runs the screen says it is loading, and a read that fails is the failed state with its retry, which keeps what the phone holds. The end asks once more each time it comes back in sight, since a change of filters or home works on the next read (ADR-062 §8): the button into What you seek leads back to it. A read that finds another day replaces the cards and says so. Later is the decision `skip`, a text button in the decision bar in every placement; a like or a fish refused with `your_turn_limit` keeps its card, and the notice says how many wait now, from the conversation list without thumbs, since a read of the queue would serve its cards again, or the `yourTurn` the round holds when that read fails. `RoundNotice` is a catalogue key, or that number.

The short pool (#99, ADR-042, ADR-062 §10): at the queue's end `ShortPool` reads what may be relaxed for today and offers each as a button with how much it widens the pool; a tap appends the first cards it added (`useRound`'s `relax`), the read after them goes on to the rest, and a relaxed card names its deal-breaker and undoes it in one tap (`unrelax`). Every card names the person's deal-breakers its person did not answer, and carries the band its entry was written with (ADR-061 §5), which `RoundScreen` hands `ProfileCardView`: nothing on the phone works one out. The likes left (#524, ADR-036 §6): each read and each decision's answer carry `likesLeft`, and the decision bar says it in words, in every placement of the buttons; nothing announces it after a decision.
