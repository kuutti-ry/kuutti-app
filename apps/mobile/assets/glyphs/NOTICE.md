# The option glyphs: sources and terms

The files under this directory are shown before an option's name in the profile (#213, ADR-019 §3). They are not under the repository's licence (`LICENSE`): each comes from Wikimedia Commons under the terms its file page states, listed here, and the party logos are the parties' marks, trademarked, shown only to name the party. A monochrome symbol is ink on transparency and is tinted with the text colour by the app; a logo keeps its colours. `zodiac/corgi.svg` is drawn for this repository and is under its licence.
