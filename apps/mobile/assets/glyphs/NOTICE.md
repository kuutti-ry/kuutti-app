# The option glyphs: sources and terms

The files under this directory are shown before an option's name in the profile (#213, ADR-019 §3), every one black and white: ink on transparency, tinted with the text colour by the app. They are not under the repository's licence (`LICENSE`). A party's mark is the party's, trademarked, shown only to name the party: five come from the party's own site (three logo files, two site icons), the rest from Wikimedia Commons and are cross-checked against the party's site on 10/10/2026, as noted. The religious symbols and the zodiac set come from Commons under the terms their file pages state. `zodiac/corgi.svg` is drawn for this repository, a placeholder until a better one, and is under its licence.

"Made black" means every colour became `#000` and every white became transparent; "inverted" means the white mark became ink and the coloured field went; a crop is a change of the viewBox only. Vihreät's and Liike Nyt's header logos are wordmarks; their marks are their site icons, the globe and the sphere.
