Every rule of how Kuutti behaves, in one list: what it is, its value today, where it lives, where it is written down, and who decided it. Some rules the maintainer chose on purpose. Others came from the private decisions log, or were assumed along the way by an agent or by habit. This register says which is which, so the maintainer can go through it area by area and keep, change or settle each rule (#396).

The four areas the maintainer named first: how many profiles a person sees and how the round is built ([Round](#round)), how many likes ([Likes and fish](#likes-and-fish)), and the hard filters ([Filters](#filters)).

## How to read a row

| Column | What it holds |
|---|---|
| Decision | The rule, in plain words. |
| Value | What it is today, in the code or in `matching_config`. |
| Where | What holds it: a `matching_config` key bare in backticks, a constant with its file, or a file. |
| Recorded in | Where it is written down, most specific first: an ADR section, an issue, a pull request, a migration. *Session* and a date when the maintainer said it only in a Claude Code session; its issue then carries a comment saying so. |
| Source | Who decided it, below. |
| Review | Empty until the maintainer marks it *keep*, *change* or *open*. A *change* becomes its own issue. |

The sources:

- **maintainer**, with the date: the maintainer chose the value it has today.
- **proposed**: written down and waiting for the maintainer, as an ADR's open point or a value of an ADR still *proposed*.
- **inherited**, with its TD number: a default from the private decisions log, never reviewed in the repository.
- **assumption**: an agent's choice, or common practice; nothing shows the maintainer chose it.
- **no record**: nothing says who chose it.

When two records disagree, the later one is the row, and its Recorded in names both.

## Keeping it true

- A pull request that changes a rule changes its row. The block under the title names the files that hold the values, and `pnpm check:docs` asks a change to one of them for its row, or for a `Still-true: docs/decisions.md` line in a commit message (ADR-047).
- `pnpm check:decisions` fails when a `matching_config` key has no row, or a row names a key no migration writes.
- A rule the maintainer settles in a session goes into its row and onto its issue in the same change.

## Where the rows come from

Read on 27 and 28/10/2026, at version 0.73.0:

- every `matching_config` key the migrations write, with the code that reads it;
- the constants in the code that set a rule a person meets, and the `shortcut:` comments that are product decisions;
- the ADRs, with every point they leave open for the maintainer, and CLAUDE.md's non-negotiable rules and product constraints;
- the maintainer's Claude Code sessions on the laptop Kuutti is built on: 110 sessions of both Claude accounts used there, from 17/09 to 27/10/2026. Every question an agent asked, with its answer, and every message the maintainer typed, with the text it answered; not the tools' output. The transcripts are private and stay on that laptop: a row takes only the decision, its date and its issue. The laptop's system was reinstalled in September 2026, so nothing before 17/09/2026 is there: the project's first five days, from 12/09, are not in it.

- **Sign-in and identity, Every sign-in goes through the bank afresh.** ADR-016 is built, and the first login on Telia's bed showed it at work, yet its status still says proposed (#203). - **Pond and gate, The default pond.** `default_pond` names the capital region but assigns nobody while two ponds exist; the seed's note that it would become null once people choose among several was never carried out.
- **Pond and gate, Pond names in the person's language.** The app names a pond in the person's language, but the public counter answers the Finnish names whatever language is asked (#174, #250): decide or drop.
- **Pond and gate, The gate's points for the maintainer.** ADR-015 §10 says the field sheet answered its points 1 and 2, while its status line, the ADR index and #250 still count all nine points as waiting. Its point 9, that the app says nothing about a round once the gate opens, is out of date: since #379 the gate card leads to today's round.
- **Profile, Gender.** ADR-010's Consequences still list the gender list and its texts as open, though the field sheet of 07/10/2026 kept the three genders and added the identity label after non-binary (#147).
- **Profile, The 30-day wait for a change of whom one seeks.** From the app a change of whom one seeks never starts the wait, so such changes are unlimited in practice, while ADR-015 §10 says the withdrawal path is no way round (#205, left open on the maintainer's word of 17/10/2026).
- **Profile, The sensitive-answers wording.** ADR-019 §4 names the draft 2026-10-draft-1; the app carries 2026-10-draft-2.
- **Profile, The card's age.** ADR-009 §6 still says the card marks the age as verified by the bank; since 12/10/2026 the card does not say it (#239, PR #268), though its contract still carries `verifiedByBank`.
- **Profile, Politics.** #213 asks for the parties in alphabetical order by the name shown; the list stands in the order of their Finnish names in every language, so in English and Swedish it is not alphabetical.
- **Profile, Religion.** ADR-019's status and its 'Not decided here' still call the religion list the maintainer's to confirm; he set its order on 10/10/2026 (#213) and its contents in a session on 12/10/2026.
- **Photos, The group-photo tip.** Since 12/10/2026 the check rejects a photo with more than one sure face, so the tip can fire only on photos a person approved on appeal.
- **Filters, The age window's starting point.** #146 gives the field sheet's default as the person's own band ±1; the app starts the window at the person's age ±5 years.
- **Filters, The age bounds.** Ages up to 130 pass the card's and the chat's contracts, but no window reaches past 99, so a person aged 100 or more is in nobody's pool.
- **Round, Rounds run on basic logic until the research partner has spoken.** impression_cap_per_day (40) is a row that nothing reads, so changing it in the panel does nothing. ADR-015 §5 says a pond whose ratio drifts is throttled by this cap, so that throttle does not exist yet.
- **Round, Production builds no rounds yet.** Until the maintainer sets how long rounds are kept, production has no rounds, so no like, fish, match or round push can happen there either.
- **Round, A round holds at most this many ordered cards.** ADR-002's Context and ADR-008 §1 still describe a round of 12 cards. The round is 10 since migration 0069.
- **Likes and fish, TD-14's contest rule waits for the research partner.** like_budget_contested (5) and contest_ratio_threshold (1.5) are rows that nothing reads, so changing them in the panel does nothing until the contest rule is built.
- **Chat, One account may send at most this many messages a minute.** ADR-031 §6 says #101 proposed 60 a minute. #101's table, migration 0054 and the code say 30.
- **Chat, Open: should the list preview the first 80 characters of the last message.** The 80 is a constant of the contract in packages/schema/src/chat.ts, not a matching_config row, though CLAUDE.md puts every tunable in matching_config. ADR-031 §6 chose so.
- **Notifications, The home screen is given the newest twenty notices.** The twenty is a constant in packages/schema/src/notifications.ts, not a matching_config row.
- **Notifications, Open: the moderation switch governs a push only.** Settings shows the moderation switch, but no code reads it: a push for a moderator's decision does not exist yet (ADR-055, Not done).
- **Notifications, Kuutti pushes three things.** Android phones get no push token until the Firebase configuration is in the native build, and production has no push setting yet, so production sends no push at all (ADR-055 Consequences).
- **Safety, Open: a shadow-banned person's read mark.** ADR-024 §7 and ADR-031 §4 say nobody learns of a shadow ban, yet the person's read mark still reaches the people they talk to, beside a missing photo and no Online (#470, open).
- **Safety, The association promises to answer within 24 hours.** #303 calls 24 the maintainer's figure, but #299, which it cites, leaves the number to him, and the moderation runbook that should state it does not exist yet.
- **Research, A changed like or pass writes its own event marked revised.** ADR-034 Open 1 still lists the revised flag as open, though the maintainer approved it in a session on 19/10/2026; the six bands of seconds stay open.
- **Break and erasure, Deleting the account erases the person's data; register again after 30 days.** The app repeats the 30 days in a constant of its own for its text (apps/mobile/src/features/identity/AccountActions.tsx), so a change needs both; the number is kept out of matching_config on purpose.
- **Break and erasure, The export lists at most the newest 1,000 rows of each history.** Older rows are left out without a word, which touches the right of access (rounds after about three years of daily use); the same thousand is repeated in apps/api/src/rewards/invitations.ts and as a literal in apps/api/src/notifications/repo.ts.
- **Break and erasure, The export lists only the newest 1,000 photo fetches and cards shown.** Daily rounds of ten cards pass a thousand rows within about a month, so an active person's export shows only the latest part of these logs.
- **Retention, Audit log entries are kept five years.** Nothing prunes the audit log and its table refuses deletes by trigger (migration 0006), so entries are kept for good, past the five years that TD-7 and .claude/rules/db.md give.
- **Limits, Cards served and photo links a day.** ADR-008 §1 and the seed's comment still size both budgets on a round of 12; the round holds 10 since migration 0069 (20/10/2026). Since ADR-056 §3 the full-size budget covers one's own photos only.
- **Limits, A person may change a consent at most ten times in 24 hours.** It counts a rolling 24 hours, unlike the Finnish day of ADR-008 §2, and each consent kind separately, though ADR-010 §4 names only the research opt-in.
- **Limits, A person, and an address, may make at most 120 requests a minute.** The request limits are configuration in the parameter store, read at boot (ADR-026 §5), not rows of matching_config; production's value may differ from the code's default.
- **Product constraints, Every tunable lives in the matching configuration table.** Numbers a person meets that read as tunables are constants in code: SUSPENSION_DAYS_MAX, REPORT_NOTE_MAX, SANCTION_NOTE_MAX, FEEDBACK_TEXT_MAX, CONSENT_CHURN_PER_DAY, the three export limits, and FOUND_THEM_RECENT_DAYS, which ADR-040 Open 2 calls the maintainer's; the request limits sit in the parameter store. The 30-day wait, k = 10, the 90 days and the twelve-month retentions are rules on purpose.
- **Product constraints, Never build real-time engagement push.** A push of a message, a match and the day's round exists since ADR-055, at most one a conversation in ten minutes and none in the quiet hours; ADR-055 is still proposed, so whether these pushes fit this line waits for the maintainer.
