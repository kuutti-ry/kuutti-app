# ADR-009: The profile registry, the plain-text rule, completeness, and one card builder

- Status: accepted; amended by ADR-019 on 2026-10-08 (every field carries its roles, the special-category flag is set on politics and religion and the gate compares the consent's version, the card carries info and hard fields only, the fields and options are the field sheet's); amended on 2026-10-08 for #149 (§1: the deal-breakers are rows of `preferences` on the registry's whitelist, under the disclose-to-filter rule); amended on 2026-10-08 for #150 (§7: the card carries the gender and the identity label, what both answered, never a field the person hides; the formats are the sheet's); amended on 2026-10-17 for #84 (§6: no card across a block, ADR-030 §2) and on 2026-10-21 for #99 (§1: a new deal-breaker lets through who did not answer until switched off, ADR-042 §8) and on 2026-10-31 for #515 (§7: a person hides any card field, ADR-019 §2; see History)
- Date: 2026-09-26
- Follows: TD-16 (the profile), TD-6 (what a verified stranger learns before a match), TD-8 (`contact_details` as a refusal reason), CLAUDE.md rules 3, 5 and 6 and Product constraints (no pre-match links; every tunable in `matching_config`), `.claude/rules/schema.md` (strict contracts; the card carries nothing about anyone else), `.claude/rules/db.md` (the erasure table), ADR-005 clause 4, ADR-006 (an automatic approval requires a face), ADR-007 §4 (the erasure join), ADR-008 §3 (the shown record and the card budget); issue #47

## Context

Nothing can be matched without a profile, and everything on a card is a decision about what a bank-verified stranger learns before a match. #47 had one line of body; this ADR fixes what the profile holds, how the free text is kept harmless, when a profile is complete, and how a card is built, so that the rounds of M4 serve cards through one path with the anti-scraping duties of #52 inside it.

## Decision

1. **The fields are a registry, not columns.** `packages/schema/src/profile-fields.ts` names each field with a kind (`single`, `multi`, `text`), its options or length cap and a `specialCategory` flag; the API validates against it at the boundary and stores the values as one `jsonb` on the `profile` row; the app builds the form and the card from it; the research snapshot of #50 reads the same keys. Adding an option is a schema change and no migration; removing one is a data change: the row keeps the value, the API reads that one field as unanswered and logs the key (never the value), and a backfill clears or maps it. Preferences about these fields are the deal-breakers (#149, TD-16): `hard` rows of `preferences` beside onboarding's two, on the fields whose `preferenceAbout` is `deal_breaker` (monogamy, kids, wanting kids, smoking, languages; religion only after the Ombudsman's answer), at most `matching_config.deal_breakers_max` of them (2 at launch, migration 0024, so a small pond is not cut to nothing; the contract's ceiling of five bounds the tunable), and each only on a field the person answered themselves: a filter on what one will not say about oneself is the one-sidedness the design document targets, and leaving a field unanswered unlocks nothing. Taking the own answer back pauses the filter (`paused`, read off the profile at every read, never stored) and answering again restores it; the rows are never deleted for it. `GET`/`PUT /preferences/deal-breakers` in the matching slice, which is handed the caller's own answers by the app rather than reading the profile table; #87 applies the rows in the pool, both ways; the research event `filter_gated_prompt_shown` waits for the partner (ADR-011). The option lists shipped are the maintainer's to change; nothing reads their meaning.
2. **A special-category value needs explicit consent first.** A field flagged `specialCategory` is refused (403 `consent_required`) unless the profile document carries the version of the consent text the person accepted (`specialCategoryConsent.version`, stored with the time it was given; a changed version resets the time, null withdraws it). #46 binds the text and its version; this issue stores them. No field in today's registry is an article 9 category on its face, so the flag ships unset and gates nothing, and a consent version sent while nothing is flagged is refused rather than stored (a row that looks like consent for a text nobody can point at would be worse than none); `seeks` (#46) and any later field that is one take the flag, and the issue that flags a field binds the version the gate compares against. The gate is a pure function tested with a registry that has such a field.
3. **Free text is short and passes the plain-text rule.** Display name (20), bio (500), prompt answers (160) and campus or guild (40) are refused with 400 `text_contact_details` when they carry an e-mail address, a URL, a phone number (seven or more digits with anything between them) or a social handle; the envelope names the rule, the log names the field, never the text. The rule is a regex list in `apps/api/src/profile/text.ts`, deliberately blunt: a false refusal costs a rephrase, a false pass costs a person their pre-match anonymity. Human review of reported text is M4.
4. **Completeness is a pure function over a snapshot** (`apps/api/src/profile/completeness.ts`): a display name, at least two approved photos (an automatic approval requires a face, ADR-006, so approved stands for "with a face"), a bio of fifty characters, a ready line in its place, or two answered prompts, and the two things onboarding sets, `seeks` and the age window. Those two come through a `PreferenceReader` that #46 implements; until then it answers null for both, so no profile is complete before onboarding exists, which is the intended order. The API returns `completeness: { complete, missing }` with every profile read; the round builder of M4 reads the same function. Changed 2026-10-05 (#56, the maintainer's decision): two photos, where this said three; a third is advice, not a condition. Changed 2026-10-13 (the maintainer, after #239: "even if empty, but placeholder - show the card"): a ready line chosen in place of a bio counts like a bio of fifty characters; the snapshot carries it, and the gate and the waitlist count read it from `profile.bio_preset`. The tips are a pure function of their own over a snapshot (`apps/api/src/profile/tips.ts`), answered next to completeness as `tips`: the kinds that apply, highest priority first; the app shows one at a time, and a dismissed one gives way to the next for the rest of the app's run. Changed 2026-10-15 (#297, the maintainer's finding): a tip is said once for the main photo and remembered on the device (`photoTipsSeen`, beside the theme and the language, never sent anywhere), whether dismissed or left on the screen, and not again while that photo stays the main one; another photo made the main one starts the record afresh, so a fish photo chosen again earns its tip again; the API's answer is unchanged. A tip never repeats what completeness asks for: a profile short of photos reads `missing`, and only one with enough of them, and fewer than `photo_tip_photos_suggested` approved or in review (3 in `matching_config`, migration 0029), is told that more would help. Label tips (`sunglasses`, `mirror_selfie`, `tight_crop`, `group_photo`, `poor_lighting`, `blurry`) read approved photos' `photo_review` signals against `matching_config` thresholds (migration 0028) and fire only when every approved photo fails that check; two more (#253, 12/10/2026) read the main photo alone, the first approved one, which is the first one others see: `revealing` when it carries a swimwear, underwear or partial-nudity label (`isRevealing`, media/label-groups.ts) and `fish` when it carries a Fish or Fishing scene label, each at `photo_tip_revealing_confidence_min` or `photo_tip_fish_confidence_min` (80, migration 0031); neither ever holds a photo back. The fish tip names the fish when the check is sure of the species (#253, 12/10/2026): the answer's `fishSpecies` is the species label on the main photo at or above `photo_tip_fish_species_min` (70, migration 0036) that the check is surest of, among the fish of Finland and nearby waters Rekognition has a label for (`FishSpecies`: pike, perch, salmon, trout, carp, eel, cod, herring, halibut; `FISH_SPECIES_LABELS`, media/label-groups.ts), and null otherwise; a species of elsewhere is never named. The app says it in the tip's own words per language. The moderators' queue leaves every tip signal out of the labels it shows: they are the owner's advice, not what a moderator decides on. Priority is fixed in `TIP_KIND_ORDER`, `revealing` first, `fish` after `group_photo`. Changed 2026-10-14 (#287): a tip about a photo was said on the profile's front page, a screen away from the photo, so the rule moved to the media slice, whose photos and labels it reads: `apps/api/src/media/tips.ts`, where `photoTips` gathers the snapshot. The tips and the fish are answered on the photo list, `PhotoList.tips` and `PhotoList.fishSpecies` on `GET /photos` and `PUT /photos/order`, and the app says them on the photos screen, by the photos, one at a time as before; `ProfileResponse` carries completeness only, and where this paragraph names `apps/api/src/profile/tips.ts` and an answer next to completeness, that is the arrangement until then.
5. **The placeholder bios are options, not text.** A person who does not want to write picks one of three canned lines (`BIO_PRESETS`, texts in `messages.yaml`); a placeholder counts for completeness like a bio of fifty characters (§4, changed 2026-10-13; until then it never counted, and two prompts were needed instead). A bio and a placeholder together is a validation error.
6. **One card builder, two callers.** `buildCard(deps, { viewer, subject })` in `apps/api/src/profile/card.ts` reads the subject's account and profile, the approved photos in the owner's order, and the age in whole years from the bank-verified year and month by the Finnish calendar (rule 3: never a day), and marks the age `verifiedByBank: true`, the only verified thing on the card. For the owner it is the preview (`GET /profile/card`), complete or not, and records nothing. For anyone else the subject must be `active` (a suspension or shadow ban holds at this gate whatever the caller filtered) and complete, and `recordCardServed` in the media slice writes the shown record for every photo on the card and counts the card against `matching_config.exposure_cards_per_day` in one statement under the viewer's advisory lock (distinct subjects shown since the Finnish day started; a subject shown again today costs nothing); above the budget, 429 `card_budget_exceeded` with `Retry-After` until midnight and a warn line. A deleted, sanctioned, incomplete or missing subject is 404. No route serves another person's card in M3: the rounds of M4 are the caller, and the builder is tested directly with a reader that says onboarding is done. Since completeness (404) is decided before the budget (429), a viewer over budget could tell a complete subject from the rest by the status once a route takes a subject id: that route answers 429 before the lookup, or 404 in both cases.
7. **What the card carries and what it does not.** The subject's account id, display name, age, the photos as `{ id, blurhash, width, height }` (bytes through `GET /photos/{id}/{variant}`, never a URL in the card, so the fetch log and the budget stay in one place, ADR-008), the self-declared gender with the identity label after it when one is set (#150; display only, never matching, ADR-019 §1), the info and hard fields of the registry (ADR-019 §2) less what the person hides (the fields of `hidden_fields`, ADR-019 §2, #515; `field` goes when `hideFromField` is set), the bio or its placeholder key, the prompts, and `shared`: the hobbies and languages both the viewer and the subject answered and the card shows, computed at build time for a viewer other than the owner and marked in words on the card ("you too"), never a count, never a score (the matching document's invariant 6). Never a like or exposure count, never anyone else's id (`rules/schema.md`). The pond name joins when #46 sets a pond. The formats are the sheet's (#150): a height in centimetres, an age as a number, every date d.M.yyyy, through `packages/i18n`'s formatters and nowhere else (`pnpm lint` holds the clients to it). The card leads with the first photo and the first prompt answer. Changed 2026-10-15 (#296): each photo carries `caption`, the key of the owner's line from `PHOTO_CAPTIONS` (`packages/schema/src/media.ts`), or null; the app renders it in the viewer's language from `photos.caption.<key>` and reads it with the photo's label. A key, never the person's words, so nothing is checked.
8. **Erasure and export.** The `profile` row goes with the account in `eraseAccount` and appears as `profile` in `AccountExport` (ADR-007 §4). Erasure now takes the account row under lock first (`FOR UPDATE`) and the profile upsert takes the same lock, so a save racing an erasure lands before the deletes or sees the tombstone; #46 does the same for its preference and consent writes, and the photo, fetch-log and session inserts, which still rely on an `EXISTS` check, follow in a small change of their own.

## Consequences

- One table (`profile`, migration 0011), one `jsonb` for the fields and one for the prompts; the seed gives the live seed account a profile so the preview has something to show against the mock IdP.
- Three new error codes with texts: `text_contact_details`, `consent_required` (text key `errors.special_category_locked`, since the catalogue routes any key with "consent" in it to the binding `legal.*` texts), `card_budget_exceeded`.
- The card route of #47 that the pointers of #48, #49 and #52 anticipated is the preview; the route that serves other people's cards is M4's, and it has nothing to decide about photos, budget or the shown record.
- Open, and the maintainer's: the option lists and their texts, which fields carry the special-category flag, the display name as a field of this issue (neither the bank nor #46 gives one), the lengths and the twelve prompts.
