# ADR-042: The short pool: coming back, relaxing a deal-breaker for the day, and who did not answer

- Status: accepted 2026-10-21 (#99, built as written here on the maintainer's decisions of that day, #99's comment of 21/10/2026; the points under "Open, the maintainer's" are his to confirm or change)
- Date: 2026-10-21
- Follows: TD-12 (relaxation, recycling, the reason on every card), TD-14 (gender-neutral rules), ADR-009 §1 (the deal-breakers), ADR-015 §6 and §7 (pool figures in steps), ADR-034 §5 and §8 (a round's completion, its one writer), ADR-035 §2 and §3 (whom a round holds, its order), ADR-037 (liked-you), CLAUDE.md rules 6, 7 and 10 and Product constraints; issues #99, #95, #97, #149, #379

## Context

TD-12 asks what a person sees when their filters leave too few people: the round says so and offers to loosen one filter at a time, by the person's tap and undone by one; earlier people come back in a fixed order, labelled; and every card says why it is there. The first cut of the builder (ADR-035) put people past both cooldowns back among new ones by their soft matches, and left the order of coming back and the relaxing to #99. On 21/10/2026 the maintainer chose: a relaxation is for the day only; only deal-breakers can be relaxed; an offer says how much it widens the pool in steps, counted when the screen opens; people who did not answer a deal-breaker's field are included by default; a like given on a relaxed card reaches its person; and the defaults of #99's comment for the rest.

## Decision

1. **Coming back, in a fixed order** (amends ADR-035 §2 and §3). The ordered places are filled from people never shown before first, by their soft matches as ADR-035 §3 orders them. When those run out, people past both cooldowns come back, labelled `seen_before`: first those whose latest decision was none or a like (left undecided, or liked and never matched), then those last passed more than `pass_cooldown_days` ago; within each, the one shown longest ago first, equal days in the hash order of ADR-035 §3. The cooldowns, the label and the order count only rounds the person opened (`opened_at`): a round built and never read showed nobody, so its people are new the next day. The edges stay as ADR-035 §2 has them.
2. **A short round** is one with fewer ordered places than `round_size`: the liked-you and fish places (ADR-037, ADR-039) do not count. Only a short round offers relaxations, and only one that would add somebody today.
3. **A relaxation is the person's own, for the day** (the maintainer, 21/10/2026). It loosens one of their own deal-breakers (ADR-009) in one of two ways: `unanswered` lets through people who did not answer the field, offered while the deal-breaker excludes them; `aside` sets the deal-breaker aside. It hangs on today's round (`round_relaxation`, migration 0076, generated; one per field and round), so tomorrow's round is built from the filters as they are stored. Nothing else is relaxed, ever: whom one seeks, the age window, the intent, the pond, a block and the hidden field hold (rule 7, ADR-015). A relaxation loosens only the person's side: whoever it lets in is still somebody whose own filters let the person through (`inEachOthersPool`, the person's deal-breakers relaxed).
4. **The offers** (`GET /rounds/{day}/relaxations`, the day in the path as every write of ADR-034 has it: an ended day answers 409 `round_expired`, one not begun 404): for a short round, every deal-breaker of the person's that is not paused and not relaxed today, each way it can be relaxed, with `more`, how much the relaxation widens the person's pool, the people of the pond in each other's pool only with it, said rounded down to a step of `waitlist_k` as ADR-015 §6 says every pool figure (`saidInStep`). It is counted when the screen asks (the maintainer's choice of 21/10/2026), unlike the gate's figure, which ADR-015 §7 has follow the night's count: a person toggling the offers learns the pool in its steps and nothing finer. Relaxations add up: each offer is counted on top of the ones in force today. The answer also lists those, so each can be undone.
5. **Relaxing is one tap** (`POST /rounds/{day}/relaxations`): the relaxation is recorded and the people it lets in today, on top of the day's relaxations in force, are added to the round, as many as the room left (`round_size` less the ordered places), by the builder's own rules: never somebody cooling down, refused, already in the round, kept apart by a hidden field, or whose like waits on the person (no ordinary card meanwhile, ADR-037); new people first, then the order of §1. They carry the reason `relaxed` and the field (`round_entries.relaxed_field`), and every relaxation of the day they came in under, the tapped one first (`relaxed_with`), are written under the round's lock by the matching slice's writer (`appendRelaxed`, ADR-034 §8), at the places after the highest the round ever gave (`rounds.last_position`, so a place an undo or an erasure freed never names somebody else the same day), and clear `completed_at` (amends ADR-034 §5: it is cleared when places are added, and set again by the decision or the undo that leaves nothing undecided). The answer is the new cards, so the phone adds them without reading the round again (each read serves every card, ADR-008). The same field relaxed again the same day is 409 `already_relaxed`; a tap counted under other relaxations than stand when it writes (another device relaxed or undid one meanwhile) is 409 `relaxations_changed`, nothing written; a relaxation the offers would not list (a field that is no deal-breaker of the person's, a paused one, or `unanswered` where those people are let through already) is 404, as is a day with no round.
6. **Undoing is one tap** (`DELETE /rounds/{day}/relaxations/{field}`): the relaxation goes, and with it today's cards that came in under it and are still undecided, a later relaxation's that needed it too; the answer names their places. A card decided stays as decided: a pass stands, a like stands and reaches its person (§7), a match stands.
7. **A like on a relaxed card reaches its person** (amends ADR-037 §1; the maintainer, 21/10/2026). It counts as the liker accepting that one person: when the liked person's round is built, the like's liked-you place asks the pool both ways with the liker's deal-breakers that card came in under (`relaxed_with`) left out, and every other filter of either side as always. The liker's deal-breaker holds for everybody else.
8. **Who did not answer** (TD-12). A deal-breaker includes people who did not answer its field unless the person switches that off: the deal-breakers screen starts a new one with the switch on. Every save names `include_unknown` for each deal-breaker, so the column's own default (false, which only onboarding's two rows take, and nothing reads there) stays, and the rows stored before keep what they were set to. Every card of the person's round names, in `notAnswered`, the person's own deal-breakers, not paused, whose field the card's person left unanswered, from the card itself (every deal-breaker field is a card field, and the card leaves an unanswered one out); the app says "Did not answer: {field}". Nothing of it reaches anybody else's card.
9. **The label on every card** is one reason, by precedence: liked you or fish, then relaxed, then seen before, then matches your filters. A relaxed card names the field it came in by (`relaxedField`).
10. **What else follows.** The person's export lists each day's relaxations (the field, the way, when, in keys and in words) and names the field of each relaxed card (`relaxedField`, in words). Erasure takes a person's relaxations with their rounds (cascade). No new research event (ADR-015 §11's precedent; the taxonomy is the research partner's, TD-5): `like`, `pass` and `profile_viewed` carry the reason `relaxed`, and `round_built`, written when the night builds, counts none.

## Consequences

- A person whose filters leave few people sees why and can widen them for the day, one deal-breaker at a time, with nothing changed for tomorrow; nobody outside the person's own filters' reach is ever shown, and nobody whose filters exclude the person.
- People come back only after everybody new: a pond of thirty people shows everybody within three days and then waits out `shown_cooldown_days`, so a small pond's rounds are short for most of a month; the panel can lower the row (#305) without code.
- A relaxed like makes the liker visible as liked-you to one person their stored deal-breaker excludes: the like was the liker's own act on that card.
- The offers' figure moves when the pool moves, in steps of `waitlist_k`, and only for the person who asks; a person widening one filter can watch their pool in those steps.
- Two devices relaxing two fields at once both add cards; the second's are counted against the room the first left, under the round's lock.

## Alternatives considered

- **A stored relaxation, kept until undone** (the first recommendation): symmetric, but the maintainer chose the day only.
- **An exact count** ("23 more"): finer than ADR-015 §6 lets any pool figure be; and the cards a tap adds are bounded by the round anyway.
- **Relaxing the age window**: no screen edits it after onboarding, and the maintainer kept the relaxation to deal-breakers.
- **Recycled people among new ones by soft matches** (the first cut): TD-12 asks for new people first.
- **Counting unopened rounds as shown**: their people were never seen, and would cool down for nothing.

## Open, the maintainer's

1. How long rounds are kept (ADR-034 Open 2): coming back reads `pass_cooldown_days` of rounds, so at least ninety days.
2. A relaxation that finds nobody new is still recorded and can be undone (§5); the app offers none that would add nobody (§2).
