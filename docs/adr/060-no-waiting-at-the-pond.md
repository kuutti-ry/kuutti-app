# ADR-060: No waiting at the pond: an admin opens matching by hand

- Status: accepted 2026-10-31 (#500, the maintainer's decisions of 2026-10-29 and, on the seek limit and gender's, of 2026-10-31, built as written here; the points under "Open, the maintainer's" are his to confirm or change; supersedes ADR-015; amends ADR-007, ADR-013, ADR-022, ADR-030, ADR-035, ADR-042 and ADR-044 where they named the gate)
- Date: 2026-10-29
- Follows: TD-10 and TD-13 (the ponds, the gate and admission: departed from here, see Context), TD-11 (rounds for complete people), TD-7 (erasure), CLAUDE.md rule 6 and Product constraints, ADR-010 §6 (activation is a rule, not a flag: the same shape for who is in a pool), ADR-013 (the counter, which stays), ADR-035 (the round builder, which reads the pond's switch now), ADR-044 (invitations hung on the gate row); issue #500

## Context

ADR-015 opened matching for a person automatically: when the people of their pond who could be shown to them, and they to those, numbered `gate_k` (30), and after a line that held the larger side of a contest while it stood over `majority_share_max` (0.6). What a person was told of it was said in steps of ten and moved only at night, and two security reviews of that record went into making those figures safe. Together with the public counter (ADR-013) this was the waitlist: register, see a place in a line and a count of how many more are needed, wait for a number.

The maintainer decided against it on 29/10/2026 (#500). Three reasons, from the day's design conversation about the like economy:

- **A pond lives on numbers.** Nothing that holds people back once an area is open earns its cost. A line for one side of a contest holds back exactly the people the pond needs most to be busy.
- **When an area opens is a judgement, not a threshold.** Whether a pond of thirty, or three hundred, is ready for its first rounds depends on who they are, what the photos look like, whether the moderators are ready: a person's call, taken once, in the panel.
- **The gate did no work the rest of the design does not do.** The hard filters both ways already decide who can be shown to whom; the like budget already keeps a like scarce; the share cap landed on one side in practice, which is the point the day's economics kept coming back to.

TD-10 and TD-13 are the log's, and the log wins where this record and the log disagree. Where it departs: TD-10's "matching opens for a person at a pool of K" and TD-13's admission by share are not built any more; TD-13's "nobody is un-admitted" holds trivially, since nobody is admitted.

## Decision

1. **A pond is open or closed, and an admin decides.** One switch per pond, `ponds.matching_open`, false until an admin sets it (a generated migration). Nothing opens or closes it by itself: no count, no threshold, no share, no night. It can be closed again, for an incident; closing builds no new round and ends no conversation, and rounds already built stay for the day.
2. **The switch is an admin's act in the panel.** `GET /admin/ponds` lists the ponds with their switch; `POST /admin/ponds/{id}/matching` with `{ open }` sets it, admins only, through a confirm step that says the effect in words. It writes one `audit_log` row, action `pond.matching`, subject the pond, detail `{ open }`: who, what, when, as every change in the panel. There is no reason text: the audit log's `detail` carries no text a person typed, and the act is its own reason; the five-year audit log is the history of when each pond opened and closed. A press that finds the switch as it asks changes nothing and writes nothing, so two admins pressing at once leave one row.
3. **Matching is open for a person when their pond is open and they are complete.** Complete as ADR-010 §6 and ADR-009 §4 read it: account `active` or `shadow_banned`, identity in good standing, gender, whom one seeks with its consent, the age window, the intent, the pond, the approved photos and the bio or prompts, the terms and the privacy consent in their current wording. No row holds it: it is read when asked, like activation (ADR-010 §6), by one reader, `jobs/pool.ts`. Whoever is in a pool: the same people, `active` only, so a shadow-banned person is in nobody's pool as before (ADR-015 §3 stands in that one point).
4. **The person is told three things.** `GET /gate` answers `{ state }` and nothing else: `incomplete` (the profile or onboarding lacks something), `closed` (complete, and the pond is not open yet), `open`. No numbers, no steps, no place, no `pending`. The route keeps its name: the gate is the pond's, and an admin opens it.
5. **The round builder reads the switch.** The night builds for every pond whose switch is on, for its complete `active` and `shadow_banned` people (ADR-035 §1 amended); the day's first read builds a missing round when the caller's pond is open and the caller is complete (§5 amended). The pond is read as before, by one reader (`subjectsOfPond`, `subjectOf`), which moves from `jobs/pond-gate.ts` to `jobs/pool.ts` and loses the gate's fields. `inEachOthersPool` and everything it asks are untouched: blocks (ADR-030 §3), deal-breakers (ADR-015 §15), intent.
6. **What goes.** `apps/api/src/pond/gate.ts` and the count of `apps/api/src/jobs/pond-gate.ts`; the `gate` table (a generated migration drops it); `matching_config` keys `gate_k` and `majority_share_max` (a custom migration deletes their rows, below; `scripts/check-decisions.ts` reads a deletion); the emptying of a gate row on a change of pond, gender or seeks (ADR-015 §8 and §9; `matching/preferences.ts`, `identity/onboarding.ts`, `pond/repo.ts`); the gate row in erasure and in the export (ADR-007: the export document has no `gate`); the gate's step in what the short pool says (ADR-042 §4: the offers' figure is said in steps of the counter's k, as before, and the function that says it lives in `packages/schema` beside `waitlistK`); the `waiting`, `pending`, `within`, `needed` and `step` of the answer and of the home screen's card, and their texts. Rows of `matching_config` otherwise follow rules/db.md: a version of a key that lives is never deleted or rewritten; a key nothing reads goes whole, by a migration that names it.
7. **Invitations hang on completeness itself.** ADR-044 §1 and §7 said "a `gate` row exists", which was the gate's count having seen a complete profile. Now a person may make an invitation while their profile and onboarding are complete, as §3 reads it (`isComplete` of `jobs/pool.ts`, the approved photos of the pool and not the onboarding status, which counts a photo still with the moderator), may redeem one only while they are not, and an invitation counts the first night its redeemer is complete. The night asks completeness of each uncounted redeemer, in its transaction, and locks the inviters of those found complete, in the same order as before. The slice is handed the reader, as the pond's route is: it reads across slices and lives with the jobs. A profile that lapses (a photo taken down) closes the way to a new invitation until it is complete again, where the gate row kept it open; the maintainer accepted this (#500). It also opens the way to a code, where the gate row kept it shut: Open 3.
8. **The counter stays.** ADR-013's figures, k and steps are unchanged. Nothing about it says "waiting" any more: it is the figure of people in an area, in the app's card and on the public route. The word goes from the texts, the identifiers, the route, the table and the key in a change of its own under #500, which amends ADR-013 then.
9. **The seek limit goes, and gender's stays** (the maintainer, 31/10/2026, #542's third decision, reversing the handoff's line that both go). As many changes of whom one seeks as one wishes: the limit is the likes a day and the fish. The limit's reason was the gate's contest exploit (ADR-015 §9, §10), which goes with the gate, so `account.seeks_changed_at`, `nextChange.seeks` and the refusal in `matching/preferences.ts` go; #205 ends with them. `gender_changed_at`, `matching_config.change_cadence_days` as a limit on gender alone, the refusal in `declareGender` and `nextChange.gender` stay, for a new reason: under #502's live queue, changing one's own gender and reading again shows who seeks that gender, which is article 9 data, and `PUT /account/gender` needs no screen. Thirty days only slows that: it is a brake until #502 looks at the class.
10. **The seed opens the ponds it writes, on every run**, as it converges on the rest of its rows, so development, previews and CI have rounds from the first start. On every run and not on insert alone, since migration 0027 writes `suomi` before any seed does: an insert alone would leave it closed. An admin's closing there lasts until the next seed, which is the seed's way everywhere it runs. Production refuses the seed; its ponds open when an admin opens them. The demo commands open every pond they populate, staging's too (Open 1).
11. **Specs.** `features/pond/gate.feature` goes. `features/pond/opening.feature` holds the switch: open by an admin and not before, the three states, who counts, another person's state never served, a move to another pond, closing again, the audit row, a moderator refused. The two pool outlines (who is in each other's pool; the sheet's examples) move to `features/matching/filters.feature`, whose function they describe. The lines of `rounds.feature`, `blocks.feature`, `filters.feature`, `reports.feature`, `pause.feature`, `preferences.feature` and `config.feature` that named the gate's count, the seek limit or `gate_k` are rewritten to the rounds or to another key.

The custom migration's SQL (rule 10), the one statement the schema DSL cannot express:

```sql
DELETE FROM "matching_config" WHERE "key" IN ('gate_k', 'majority_share_max');
```

## Consequences

- Less is said about people than before: the per-person figures in steps, their first ask, the line's places and the race between a count and a change of declaration (ADR-015 §6 to §9 and "What stays possible") are gone with the gate. What is left to infer from the gate's answer is one bit, whether a person's pond is open, which the pond's own card says to everybody in it.
- The home screen's card has one sentence per state and no numbers. The counter's card is as it was. The screen-reader walk of the home screen follows the change (`.claude/rules/mobile.md`).
- Every persona of the demo now has matching open, in the ponds the demo commands open, so the home card leads each to a round and the one offer of notifications, where only Iida's did. The Maestro flows run on the pull request that carries this.
- Production builds no rounds until the maintainer has set how long they are kept (ADR-034 Open 2, ADR-035 Open 1). Opening a pond there tells its people that matching is open, and no round comes, so the panel's confirm step says so.
- A move to another pond takes effect at once, where it used to empty the gate row until the night: somebody who moves to an open pond is told open and gets a round the same day, and a person with today's round who moves to another open pond has the day's offers read from the new pond, in the counter's steps. Every card still passes `inEachOthersPool`, within `round_size`. The pond's own choice is what #553 and #514 take up.
- The nightly jobs lose `pond-gate`; `round-builder` runs after the counter and the invitations' count. `docs/architecture/sequence-night.md` and `components.md` are redrawn (ADR-047).
- The export document loses its `gate` section and `seeksChangedAt` (ADR-007), and erasure one row of its table (`.claude/rules/db.md`). Neither was new to anybody, and the privacy notice names neither.
- `matching_config` loses two keys and the register two rows for them; the register's Pond section is rewritten.
- ADR-015 is superseded and kept for its reasoning, the first record to be. ADR-007, ADR-013, ADR-022, ADR-030, ADR-035, ADR-042 and ADR-044 carry a line each where they named the gate.
- What is not built, and would be a decision of its own: a rate on admission (ruled out, #500: the pond lives on numbers), a per-pond threshold in the panel that opens the switch by itself (ruled out: a person decides), parent ponds that widen a thin pool (ADR-015 §2's "later decision" stands, in ADR-010 §12's terms).

## Open, the maintainer's

1. Whether staging's ponds are opened by hand once after each reset, or `demo:reset` opens them. Meanwhile the demo commands open every pond, since the personas' stories need rounds (ADR-014).
2. Whether the panel's front page should show a closed pond with people in it as something that waits. Meanwhile it does not: the Ponds screen is where the switch is.
3. Whether a person whose profile lapsed may redeem an invitation. Completeness is read when asked, so nothing remembers that a profile was once complete, and the rule "before their profile first passes" (ADR-044 §6) now reads "while it is not complete". One redemption ever bounds it, and it counts the night the profile is complete again. Meanwhile so; a date on the account that it first became complete would close it, at the cost of a column that nothing else asks for.
