# Architecture decision records

A record here holds one decision about how Kuutti is built or run: the context, the decision in numbered points, what follows from it, and what it leaves open. CLAUDE.md's Decisions section says when one is due: a change of identity, upload, event flow, data retention or infrastructure carries an ADR, or a citation of the TD it follows, in the same change. A later change to a decision is made in the record itself, as a numbered amendment or a line under its History, and its status line says so. A History line that only records how the decision was carried out (a cut-over on staging, a check that now runs) changes no point and leaves the status line as it is.

Every record opens with the same list: `Status`, `Date` (when it was written), `Follows` (what it rests on: TD numbers, other records, CLAUDE.md's rules, issues). The status reads:

- **proposed**: written, and usually built as written; accepting it is the maintainer's, and the parenthesis says how far it is built and what waits on him. The status changes when he has answered.
- **accepted**, sometimes with the day it was accepted: the decision stands; a parenthesis names whose decision it was, or what it leaves open: the maintainer's to confirm, accept or change, or a `shortcut:` and what lifts it.
- **amended** on a date or over a range of dates, **by ADR-NNN**, or **by §N** (a numbered amendment): points were changed or added later, in this record; the parenthesis names them, with their issue where there is one, and the History or the point itself says how.
- **superseded by ADR-NNN**: replaced, and kept for its reasoning. No record is, so far.

A record that changes what a view of `docs/architecture/` draws (a module, an environment, a flow across the system, a trust boundary) redraws that view in the same pull request (#397, ADR-047).

The TD numbers are the technical decisions log, TD-1 to TD-19, which lives in the maintainers' private planning documents (CLAUDE.md); where a record and the log disagree, the log wins. The repository cannot show the log, so this page maps each TD number to the public records that cite it, in their `Follows` line or their text: a reader of "TD-6" in CLAUDE.md finds here what to read. A TD that no record cites says "no ADR", with where the repository names it.

`pnpm check:adr-index` (`scripts/check-adr-index.ts`, in CI) fails when a record is missing from the first table, when two records share a number, when a row names a file that does not exist, or when a row's status is not its record's Status line word for word: a new record or a changed status changes this page in the same pull request. The TD columns are not checked.
