# The demo photos: the prompt sheet (#142, ADR-014 §7)

What goes into `kuutti-ry/kuutti-app-demo-photos` as release `v1`, and how it is made. The pictures are generated by a non-local image model the maintainer drives (Gemini's image model keeps one person consistent over two or three pictures; ChatGPT's works too). Nobody real: no stock photo, no scrape, no team member, no public figure, and no prompt that names one. Every file carries its PROVENANCE line. The app's repository holds the manifest and the checksums, never a picture (a test refuses binaries under `packages/db/src/seed/`).

## The sets

| path | for | pictures | what the pictures show |
|---|---|---|---|
| `faces/aino/1.jpg` … `3.jpg` | Aino's camera roll (the live upload of the storyboard) | 3 | one woman of about 33, Finnish, warm and ordinary: a portrait in daylight, one outdoors in a wool coat, one laughing at a table |
| `faces/sanna/1.jpg` … `3.jpg` | Sanna's profile (complete) | 3 | one woman of about 49, an architect who birdwatches: a portrait, one on a forest path with binoculars, one at a drawing board |
| `faces/noa/1.jpg` … `2.jpg` | Noa's profile (incomplete: two of three) | 2 | one non-binary person of about 43, a doctoral researcher who climbs: a portrait, one at a climbing wall |
| `faces/kerttu/1.jpg` … `3.jpg` | Kerttu's profile | 3 | one woman of about 46, Swedish-speaking, by the sea with a book and coffee: a portrait, one on a shore, one in a café |
| `faces/pool/001.jpg` … `060.jpg` | the synthetic population, drawn by seed | 60 | sixty different adults of 18 to 80, every gender, the mix of a Finnish city, one picture each, each a plausible first profile photo |
| `negatives/no-face.jpg` | the moderation queue | 1 | a landscape, no person: the automatic check finds no face and queues it (ADR-006) |
| `negatives/several-people.jpg` | the moderation queue | 1 | three people in one picture: more than one face |
| `negatives/text.jpg` | the moderation queue | 1 | a picture that is mostly a sign or a page of text |
| `negatives/exif-location.jpg` | the stripping of EXIF (rule 4) | 1 | any picture of the pool, re-saved with an invented GPS position and camera in its EXIF; the pipeline's output has none |

Ages follow the personas' codes (`packages/db/src/seed/personas.ts`); the clothes, places and hobbies follow their stories (`packages/db/src/seed/histories.ts`). Nothing in a picture reads as a real place name, a brand, a licence plate, a phone number or an address.

## The prompts

One prompt per set, then the model's own "same person" continuation for the second and third picture. Say the age, the setting and the mood; never a name of a real person, a celebrity, an actor or "looks like". Ask for 1200×1600 (3:4) JPEG, natural light, no text in the picture, no watermark. Keep the prompt id (`p-aino-1`, `p-pool-017`…) in the PROVENANCE line.

```
p-sanna-1   A portrait of a Finnish woman around 49, short grey-blond hair, light eyes, a calm half smile, outdoors in soft autumn daylight, wearing a dark green wool jumper. Photorealistic, 3:4, natural light, no text.
p-sanna-2   The same woman on a forest path in Nuuksio-like woods, binoculars around her neck, looking slightly away from the camera.
p-sanna-3   The same woman at a drawing board in a bright office, pencil in hand, turning to the camera.
```

Write the other sets the same way. For the pool, vary age, gender, hair, skin, glasses, setting and season; keep every picture a single person, face clearly visible, nothing suggestive, nothing that reads as a minor (the youngest are 18, and look it).

## Per file

- A `PROVENANCE.md` line: `faces/sanna/1.jpg — <model and version>, <date dd/mm/yyyy>, prompt p-sanna-1, held under <the model provider's terms, by name and date>`.
- A `manifest.json` entry: `{ "path": "faces/sanna/1.jpg", "sha256": "<hex>", "purpose": "persona:sanna" }`; purposes are `persona:<key>`, `pool`, `negative:<name>`. `pnpm demo:assets -- --manifest <dir>` in the app's repository writes it from a directory of pictures, and the same file is committed there as `packages/db/src/seed/photos-manifest.json`.
- `LICENSE`: the terms the pictures are held under, and that they are for the demo of this project only, never published, never in a store listing or a screenshot that leaves the team.

## Before the release

- The maintainer has looked at every picture once: nobody real, nobody young, nothing in the background.
- `manifest.json` lists every file, and no file is unlisted.
- Tag `v1`; `pnpm demo:assets` in the app's repository downloads and verifies it.
