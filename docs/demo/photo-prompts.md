# The demo photos: the prompt sheet (#142, ADR-014 §7)

What goes into `kuutti-ry/kuutti-app-demo-photos` as release `v1`, and how it is made. The pictures are generated by a non-local image model the maintainer drives. Nobody real: no stock photo, no scrape, no team member, no public figure, and no prompt that names one. Every file carries its PROVENANCE line. The app's repository holds the manifest and the checksums, never a picture (a test refuses binaries under `packages/db/src/seed/`).

## The model and its settings

Chosen 09/10/2026.

- **Nano Banana Pro** (`gemini-3-pro-image`) in Google AI Studio, one chat per persona. Of Google's image models its people look the most like photographs. The fallback is **Nano Banana 2.1** (generally available since 06/10/2026): cheaper, and better at keeping one person's face over several pictures, so it is the one to switch to when Pro's face drifts between a persona's pictures, and the one for the pool of sixty.
- Settings: aspect ratio **3:4**, resolution **2K** (1792×2400; 1K is too small to survive the downscale well, 4K costs more for nothing). Temperature, thinking and the safety settings stay at their defaults.
- AI Studio rather than the Gemini app: the app has no control over ratio or resolution, and on the free and Pro plans it marks the corner of the picture. Every picture of either carries Google's invisible SynthID watermark, which is right: it says the picture is generated.
- Picture 1 from its full prompt; pictures 2 and 3 in the same chat, so the model has picture 1 in front of it. If the face drifts, open a new chat, attach picture 1, and paste the continuation prompt.
- Make two or three of each and keep the best. Throw away: anybody who could be read as under 18, anybody who resembles a person you know of, extra or melted fingers, a second face anywhere (a café guest, a reflection, a poster), legible text, a logo, a plate, a house number, and the glossy look of an advertisement.

Nothing in a picture reads as a real place name, a brand, a licence plate, a phone number or an address.

## The prompts

Each block is one paste. Pictures 4 and 5 are imperfect on purpose, because a real camera roll is: a selfie at arm's length, soft focus, a tilted frame, dim light; the face stays visible, since the check needs one. A first picture ends with the paragraph that makes it look like a phone's photo and not an advertisement; a continuation starts by holding the face and ends with the short form of the same. Keep the prompt id in the PROVENANCE line. For the pool, write the prompts the same way and vary age, gender, hair, skin, glasses, setting and season; every picture a single adult, face clearly visible, nothing suggestive, the youngest 18 and looking it.

## From the model to the release

AI Studio downloads PNG. Keep the downloads in a working folder outside the release checkout, named after their place in it (`faces/aino/1.png`, `negatives/no-face.png`, `noa-3.png`): the manifest reads every picture under `faces/` and `negatives/`, so a PNG left next to its JPEG is a second picture of the persona.

1. Convert into the release checkout at 1200×1600, JPEG quality 88 (from the working folder; `sips` ships with macOS):
   ```bash
   for f in faces/*/*.png negatives/*.png; do mkdir -p ~/Developer/kuutti-app-demo-photos/"$(dirname "$f")"; sips -s format jpeg -s formatOptions 88 -Z 1600 "$f" --out ~/Developer/kuutti-app-demo-photos/"${f%.png}.jpg"; done
   ```
2. Noa's third picture, with its invented position (open sea in the Gulf of Finland, nobody's address) and camera (`brew install exiftool` once):
   ```bash
   sips -s format jpeg -s formatOptions 88 -Z 1600 noa-3.png --out ~/Developer/kuutti-app-demo-photos/negatives/exif-location.jpg
   ```
   ```bash
   exiftool -overwrite_original -GPSLatitude=59.9 -GPSLatitudeRef=N -GPSLongitude=24.75 -GPSLongitudeRef=E -Make=Invented -Model="Demo Camera 1" ~/Developer/kuutti-app-demo-photos/negatives/exif-location.jpg
   ```
3. In the app's repository, `pnpm demo:assets -- --manifest ~/Developer/kuutti-app-demo-photos` writes `packages/db/src/seed/photos-manifest.ts`; the same list goes into the release as `manifest.json`.

## Per file

- A `PROVENANCE.md` row: `faces/sanna/1.jpg`, what it is, `Nano Banana Pro (gemini-3-pro-image), Google AI Studio, prompt p-sanna-1`, the terms by name and date (the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service of that day), the date dd/mm/yyyy.
- A `manifest.json` entry: `{ "path": "faces/sanna/1.jpg", "sha256": "<hex>", "purpose": "persona:sanna" }`; purposes are `persona:<key>`, `pool`, `negative:<name>`.
- `LICENSE`: the terms the pictures are held under, and that they are for the demo of this project only, never published, never in a store listing or a screenshot that leaves the team.

## Before the release

- The maintainer has looked at every picture once: nobody real, nobody young, nothing in the background.
- `manifest.json` lists every file, and no file is unlisted.
- Tag `v1`; `pnpm demo:assets` in the app's repository downloads and verifies it.
